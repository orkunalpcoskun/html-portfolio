I was born in Brooklyn, NY, USA in 1999. But due to certain events that took place I had to return to Turkey with my parents after 2 years of living in New York. I grew up in Istanbul, spent most of my life there. I went to elementary and middle school in Bahçeşehir Süleyman demirel primary school. After that, I enrolled to a vocational high school in web development field, which was the reason I had developed interest in coding. I also learned valuable information about computer hardware and software in general. That also shaped my future.

After graduating from high school in 2017, I enrolled in higher education in Beykoz univesity, digital game design department. I always had passion for video games, I still play games in my free time. So I thought why not combine my passion with my future career. During my university years, I developed several games alone and with teams. Thanks to my teachers I got to experience different disciplines of game development such as 3D modeling, animation, level design, programming etc. In my 3rd year I took advantage of the Erasmus+ program and went to study in Poland for one semester. Despite the difficulties, both in my control and out of my control, such as coronavirus pandemic, I geniunely enjoyed my time there. I got to meet new people from different cultures and backgrounds. It was a great experience for me.

Later on, after my graduation in 2021 I was presented with a nice job opportunity in the field I was totally unfamiliar with. I worked as IT specialist for a media company for about 2 years. Throughout my work life I got to know about corporate life, teamwork and communication. I learned a lot about computer networks. I would also like to thank my coworkers because of their support and help I wouldn't be able to do my job. However, deep down I knew that this was not the field I wanted to be in. So I decided to change my career path and want to become a full stack web developer. This is the reason why I'm taking this full stack web development bootcamp course right now. So far I'm doing great and I'm determined to achieve my goals. My ultimate dream is to move back to USA, and work in a tech company where I can demonstrate my skills and contribute to the team that I will be in.

I will update my bio in the future. I can't wait to see how this turns out.
